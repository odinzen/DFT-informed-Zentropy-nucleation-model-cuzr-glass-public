Supplementary code and data for Hyalos
======================================
Manuscript: Glass-forming ability in Cu-Zr and Zr-Cu-Al with Hyalos: liquidus-calibrated Zentropy
nucleation kinetics

Contents
  zentropy_driving_force.py  Debye Grueneisen crystalline free energy from DFT bulk
                             moduli; two configuration Zentropy supercooled liquid;
                             driving force calibrated to the liquidus.
  hyalos_kinetics.py         Viscosity through two measured points per alloy, the Turnbull
                             coefficient, and the classical nucleation model; every
                             published diameter comes from here.
  nucleation_zentropy.py     Locates the TTT nose; critical cooling rate and casting
                             diameter; figures.
  sensitivity.py             Sensitivity of the prediction to each input (Fig S1).
  hyalos_reality.py          Assessed crystal phases as separate nuclei, diffusion limited
                             growth, and a liquid with a spectrum of configurations
                             (Section S12). Needs pycalphad.
  dft_appendix_a/            Structures, VASP and Quantum ESPRESSO inputs, equation of
                             state and Debye fitter, and the Zentropy handoff script.
  config2/                   The config-2 free-energy campaign (see its README).

Requirements: python 3, numpy, scipy, matplotlib. No production DFT or molecular
dynamics is needed at the point of prediction. The package in dft_appendix_a computes
the one remaining first principles input on the user's own machine.
